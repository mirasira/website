🏷️ STag Marker Generator

The fiducial arucogen doesn't make. Runs entirely in your browser.

STag markers encode 48 bits as a "sunburst" ring of dots inside a black square + white locator circle, decoded with 4-fold rotational lookup against a fixed codeword dictionary — no algorithmic BCH decode. See the bbenligiray/stag reference implementation and the original paper (Benligiray, Topal, Akinlar, 2019).

Codeword dictionaries are the exact HD11–HD23 tables shipped in the stag repo (max-ID per library shown once loaded).
